## Suggested Review Flow

1. Download `aurora-commerce-launch.mppplan`.
2. Open the file in MPP Viewer.
3. Review `Portfolio`, `Dashboard`, `Plan Builder`, `Agile Board`, `Gantt Chart`, `Resources`, `Calendar`, `Status Center`, `Earned Value`, and `Workload`.
4. Use `File > Close Window` or Command-W to close the document window.
5. Use `MPP Viewer > Quit MPP Viewer` or Command-Q to quit the app.
