# Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 8, 2026

MPP Viewer is a macOS app for opening, reviewing, and working with Microsoft Project files and native project plans.

## Data Collection

MPP Viewer does not collect, sell, share, or transmit personal data.

The app does not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, tracking SDKs, or remote telemetry.

## Local File Access

MPP Viewer runs locally on your Mac. Project files, imported data, app settings, and generated project-plan data remain on your device unless you choose to export or share them yourself.

The app accesses files you select in order to open, convert, preview, edit, save, or export project information.

## Network Access

MPP Viewer does not require an account and does not send project files or project metadata to a server.
