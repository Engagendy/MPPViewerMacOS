## Description
Planroom is a fast, native project planner for macOS. Build a plan, schedule it, staff it, and track it — all in one focused workspace, with no accounts and no cloud required. Open Microsoft Project (.mpp) files or start fresh with the native plan format.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Plan builder — a keyboard-first grid for rapid entry. Copy, paste, duplicate, insert above/below and reorder rows, or paste rows straight from Excel and Numbers.
• Interactive Gantt — drag bars, link dependencies, roll up phases, and read the critical path and baseline variance at a glance.
• Holidays, events & leave — record company holidays, observances (like Ramadan) and per-person leave, then overlay them on the timeline to spot every conflict. Toggle overlays on or off; show leave as row bars or full-height columns.
• Resources & workload — manage staffing and rates, and see allocation and time off together so over-allocation never sneaks up on you.
• Schedule, milestones & timeline — clean, presentation-ready views of the same plan.
• Earned value & analysis — EVM metrics, baselines, dependency explorer, resource risks and validation.
• Beautiful exports — export the Gantt, Workload and Events views to branded PDF and scalable SVG, with dates and overlays included.
• Import & templates — bring in tasks, assignments, dependencies, constraints, baselines, resources and leave from CSV or Excel using ready-made templates.

Planroom keeps your data on your Mac. It’s built with native macOS technologies for speed and a familiar, distraction-free experience.

## What's New (v2.6.3)
Holidays, events & leave come to the timeline — plus faster editing and richer exports.

• Holidays, events & leave: add company holidays, observances (e.g. Ramadan) and per-resource leave, and overlay them on the Gantt. Toggle each on or off, and show leave as per-row bars or full-height columns. Hover any title to see its exact dates.
• Events & Leave workspace: a new sidebar screen with a details list beside a dedicated timeline — edit everything in one dialog, and export it to PDF or SVG.
• Workload now shows resource leave alongside allocation, so over-allocation and time off are visible together.
• Plan builder: copy and paste rows (including straight from Excel), duplicate a task with the same dates and assignments, insert rows above or below, and reorder more smoothly.
• Exports: PDF and SVG are now branded with Planroom and include start/end dates and your holiday/event/leave overlays. Long labels wrap onto a second line and no longer overlap.
• Templates & import for events and resource leave — download a CSV/Excel template (pre-filled with your resources for leave) and import in one step.
• Performance: the Resources screen is noticeably smoother.
• The app now shows “Planroom” consistently in the menu bar, Dock and About panel.
